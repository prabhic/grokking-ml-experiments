LLM first principles · Single-Head Attention Playground

Single-Head Attention makes one hidden mechanism visible.

QKᵀ → softmax → weighted V. This playground keeps the moving parts small so the calculation can be followed from input to output.

Read the playground

Move the control and watch the highlighted result change. The four stages describe the mechanism in the same order as the code.

What this isolates

QKᵀ → softmax → weighted V. The toy values are teaching data; real models use larger vectors, matrices, vocabularies, and datasets.

Why it matters

This mechanism is one link in the first-principles chain from text to tokens, vectors, attention, prediction, and learning.

Terms

input is what enters the operation. transform is the calculation. output is what the next mechanism receives.

A short history

Neural attention was introduced as a soft alignment idea in Bahdanau, Cho, and Bengio's 2014 sequence-to-sequence work. The Transformer paper, Attention Is All You Need (2017), made scaled dot-product self-attention the central building block used by modern LLMs. Bahdanau et al. ↗ Transformer paper ↗

Question & answer

Does a key mean the model matches one key and selects that key's value? Not exactly. The query is compared with every key. Softmax turns all those similarity scores into weights, and the output is a weighted mixture of all the corresponding value vectors. If one key matches much better, its value can dominate—but the mechanism is not restricted to one exact match.
