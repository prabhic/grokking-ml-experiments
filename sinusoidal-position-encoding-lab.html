Sinusoidal encoding · sine · cosine · fixed positions

Position becomes a pattern of waves.

The original Transformer can calculate a position signal using sine and cosine functions. Every position gets a deterministic vector, so the model does not need a learned position table.

Alternating channels

Even dimensions use sine; odd dimensions use cosine. Each dimension oscillates at a different frequency, giving every position a distinctive pattern.

Fixed means no training

The signal is calculated from a formula. Extending the sequence means evaluating the same formula at a new position, rather than adding a new table row.

Why waves?

Sine and cosine vary smoothly. Nearby positions receive related signals, and combinations of frequencies can identify position.

Formula

PE(pos, 2i) = sin(pos / 10000^(2i/d))
PE(pos, 2i+1) = cos(pos / 10000^(2i/d))

Terms

fixed encoding
Calculated by a rule, not learned as table weights.
frequency
How quickly a channel’s wave changes.
channel
One coordinate of the position vector.
