What this isolates

Logits become token probabilities. The toy values are teaching data; real models use larger vectors, matrices, vocabularies, and datasets.

Why it matters

This mechanism is one link in the first-principles chain from text to tokens, vectors, attention, prediction, and learning.

Terms

input is what enters the operation. transform is the calculation. output is what the next mechanism receives.

A short history

The word logit was coined by Joseph Berkson in 1944 as the log-odds of the logistic function. The softmax name comes from John S. Bridle's 1989–1990 neural-network classification work, which connected exponential output scores with class probabilities. The temperature knob borrows the Boltzmann distribution from statistical mechanics; in modern LLMs it is applied to the final logits at decoding time, not learned by the model.
