A short history

The skip connection became famous through He, Zhang, Ren, and Sun's ResNet (2015), which showed that letting each layer learn a residual x + F(x) instead of a full replacement made very deep networks trainable. Highway networks by Srivastava, Greff, and Schmidhuber (2015) proposed gated skips at the same time, and the idea echoes the constant error carousel inside the 1997 LSTM. The Transformer (2017) wraps every attention and feed-forward sublayer in one, which is why an LLM's hidden state is often called the residual stream. ResNet ↗ Highway networks ↗ Transformer paper ↗
