Embedding values · vector scale · normalization

A bigger number does not mean a stronger meaning.

Raw embedding values depend on the model and training process. Multiplying every coordinate by the same positive number makes a vector longer without changing its direction.

Why −1 to +1?

The earlier dimensions lab uses that range to keep bars readable. It is a display choice, not a universal rule for real embeddings.

Scale is not meaning

A coordinate of 0.8 is not automatically more meaningful than one of 0.2. Meaning lives in the complete pattern.

Normalization

Unit normalization divides every coordinate by the vector length. Coordinates shrink, the length becomes 1, and direction stays.

Formula

cosine(kA,B) = cosine(A,B) for k > 0
cosine = dot / (|A| × |B|)
