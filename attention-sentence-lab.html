LLM first principles · Attention Sentence Playground

Attention Sentence makes one hidden mechanism visible.

A selected token distributes attention across a sentence. This playground keeps the moving parts small so the calculation can be followed from input to output.

Read the playground

Move the control and watch the highlighted result change. The four stages describe the mechanism in the same order as the code.

What this isolates

A selected token distributes attention across a sentence. The toy values are teaching data; real models use larger vectors, matrices, vocabularies, and datasets.

Why it matters

This mechanism is one link in the first-principles chain from text to tokens, vectors, attention, prediction, and learning.

Terms

input is what enters the operation. transform is the calculation. output is what the next mechanism receives.

Try this

  1. Move the control slowly and narrate each stage.
  2. Press Example to jump to a contrasting state.
  3. Reset and reproduce the change from the beginning.

A short history

Soft attention became prominent in sequence-to-sequence translation through Bahdanau, Cho, and Bengio's 2014 work. The 2017 Transformer paper then made attention over token sequences the central operation behind modern language models. Bahdanau et al. ↗ Transformer paper ↗
