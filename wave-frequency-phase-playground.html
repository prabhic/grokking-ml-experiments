Wave ↔ Numbers Playground

This playground connects four views of the same idea: circle → smooth wave → sampled numbers → number sequence as a signal.
x(t) = A · sin(2πft + φ)
Core insight:
1. A smooth wave can be turned into a sequence of numbers by sampling its height at regular times.
2. A sequence of numbers can be drawn across time and treated as a signal — a wave-like changing quantity.
So, wave and number-sequence are two views of the same thing.

3. A wave can be represented as numbers (samples)

Meaning: each number is the wave height at one sampled time instant.

4. A sequence of numbers can be thought of as a signal / wave-like shape

Meaning: even if these numbers are not a perfect sine wave, once placed across time, they become a discrete signal.
Frequency Changes how fast the point rotates. Faster rotation means more wave cycles per second.
Phase Specifies the starting angle. It says where the wave begins in its cycle.
Numbers as signals A list like [2, 4, 1, -1, 3] is not "just numbers" — it can be viewed as values changing over time.
Suggested path to learn: first understand the circle and phase, then see the smooth wave, then see how it becomes a sequence of numbers, and finally notice how any number sequence can itself be interpreted as a signal.

A short history: Joseph Fourier developed trigonometric series in his work on heat, published in expanded form in 1822. That work helped establish the broader idea that changing phenomena can be represented as combinations of waves. Read a historical overview ↗
