RoPE · rotation · query/key attention

Position can turn a vector while keeping its length.

Rotary position encoding applies a position-dependent rotation to pairs of query and key coordinates. Attention then compares the rotated vectors, so relative position can affect their score.

Rotation is not addition

RoPE changes the orientation of query and key vectors using a rotation matrix. It does not append a new position vector or simply add one.

Length survives

A pure rotation preserves vector length. The coordinates change, but the distance from the origin stays constant.

Why queries and keys?

Attention scores compare queries with keys. Rotating both according to position makes those comparisons sensitive to relative offsets.
